# Review Checklist

## Revenue

- Does the change map to a real funnel stage?
- Is the expected commercial effect measurable?
- Is there a cheaper/smaller path to the same result?

## Truth and compliance

- Are all identities, claims, cases and metrics truthful?
- Does the change comply with current platform rules?
- Does it avoid duplicate accounts, decoy bids, spam and prohibited auto-actions?

## Security

- Are secrets excluded from code, logs and artifacts?
- Are permissions minimal?
- Are client and account data protected?

## Reliability

- Are retries, deduplication and failure states handled where needed?
- Can the system recover without losing leads or messages?
- Are destructive actions prevented?

## Delivery

- Were targeted tests/checks run?
- Is `main` and production unchanged?
- Is the work in a branch/draft PR?
- Were `DECISIONS.md`, `GOAL_PROGRESS.md` and `NEXT_ACTION.md` updated when necessary?
