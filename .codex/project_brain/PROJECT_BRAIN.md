# Project Brain

## Project identity

- Name: Antonov Digital Freelance Revenue Engine
- Brand: Antonov Digital
- Team: two founders
- Purpose: continuously create legitimate paid freelance opportunities with minimal manual involvement before delivery
- Initial platform focus: Freelancehunt
- Future channels: direct email, other freelance platforms, LinkedIn and inbound channels, added only under their current rules

## Core business outcome

The project succeeds only when it creates a repeatable path to funded work and received revenue.

Primary outcome:

`NET_FUNDED_REVENUE`

Supporting outcomes:

- funded projects
- completed projects
- positive reviews
- repeat clients
- response rate
- qualified-reply rate
- proposal-to-funded conversion
- net profit after platform fees, tax and delivery cost
- time from opportunity to first response

## Team model

### Founder 1

- management
- marketing campaigns
- client acquisition
- sales communication
- scoping and project coordination
- selected development work

### Founder 2

- primary development
- technical implementation
- engineering support during scoping and delivery

The profile must never claim a larger team unless that team exists and can actually deliver the work.

## Service discovery policy

At launch, do not assign fixed priority to service directions. Test all relevant services using real opportunities and data:

- AI agents
- business automation
- Telegram bots
- CRM and internal tools
- integrations and APIs
- websites and landing pages
- web applications and MVPs
- e-commerce
- AI content, images and video workflows
- audio processing
- SEO, GEO and Google Business Profile work
- data collection, monitoring and reporting
- other work that the team can truthfully complete

The system may later allocate more effort to a direction only after evidence shows stronger demand, conversion, profit, speed or repeatability.

## Price and workload policy

There is no fixed minimum project price.

Small or discounted work may be accepted for:

- immediate cash
- first platform reviews
- rating and trust
- a valuable public case
- entry into a new service category
- a realistic repeat-client opportunity

Low price is not permission for unlimited scope. Every accepted project must still have a defined result, boundaries, revision policy, payment path and fraud-risk check.

The user is available approximately 08:00–21:00 Europe/Kyiv for intervention and can work up to 18 hours during high-demand periods. Customer deadlines must use conservative capacity rather than assuming 18 hours every day.

## Language and communication

The system may communicate in Ukrainian, Russian or English according to the client and platform context.

The founder can participate in English calls and record short video explanations or solution walkthroughs when commercially useful.

## Autonomy model

The system should minimize routine questions and complete ordinary reversible work independently under documented rules.

The system must not interpret autonomy as permission to:

- break platform rules
- create deceptive identities
- send spam
- bypass verification
- invent facts
- expose secrets
- promise unapproved legal, payment or production commitments

There is no destructive “self-deletion” behavior. A non-performing strategy is disabled, reviewed and replaced. Data, accounts and working infrastructure are preserved.

## Lead handoff threshold

The preferred handoff to delivery occurs when the following are recorded:

- client identity and source
- exact requested outcome
- agreed scope
- price or funded milestone
- deadline
- revision boundaries
- communication history
- references and assets
- risks and assumptions
- next promised action

## Project stages

### Stage 0 — Brain and governance

Create the durable repository context, platform policy, email map, metrics and operating protocols.

### Stage 1 — Profile and portfolio

Build the truthful Antonov Digital profile, service presentation, portfolio cases and supporting assets.

### Stage 2 — Opportunity ingestion

Collect permitted platform alerts and direct leads, normalize them and prevent duplicates.

### Stage 3 — Qualification and proposal

Evaluate fit, scope, risk, price logic and relevant evidence; create personalized proposals.

### Stage 4 — Negotiation and handoff

Manage ordinary communication, track commitments and hand funded projects to delivery.

### Stage 5 — Optimization

Use real funnel data to improve service positioning, case selection, proposal strategy, follow-ups and channel allocation.
