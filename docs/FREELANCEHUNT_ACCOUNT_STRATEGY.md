## One-account and one-mailbox strategy

Use one active adult-owned Freelancehunt account and one adult-owned operational mailbox for:

- account registration
- security and service notices
- new-project notifications
- client-thread notifications
- permitted Gmail ingestion by the internal revenue agent

There is no notification mirror or forwarding mailbox in the current architecture.

The agent may read permitted notification emails, score opportunities and draft proposals. The adult owner performs every action inside Freelancehunt.

## Rejected approach

- duplicate freelancer accounts
- fake or low-quality decoy accounts
- coordinated weak proposals
- a forwarding mailbox as a second platform identity

Concentrate rating, reviews and portfolio trust in the one approved adult-owned account.

## Legal contrast strategy

Create contrast inside one legitimate proposal:

- diagnose the real problem in the opening
- show one directly relevant case
- present a concise execution plan
- identify one risk the client may have missed
- offer a minimal and a recommended scope when useful
- attach a short video or demo for high-value opportunities

## A/B testing strategy

- Generate two or three internal proposal variants.
- Score them for specificity, truthfulness, proof and risk.
- Send only the strongest version through the adult-owned account.
- Test variants across comparable opportunities over time.
- Track replies, qualified replies, funded projects and net value.
