# Freelancehunt Account Strategy

## Decision

Launch with one legitimate primary freelancer account in an approved legal format.

## Rejected approach

Do not create a second fake or low-quality account to submit a weak proposal before the main account.

Reasons:

- prohibited duplicate-account and truthfulness risk
- potential loss of both accounts and funds
- coordinated deception of the client
- fragmented rating, reviews and portfolio trust
- additional subscriptions, verification and operational complexity
- no reliable incremental conversion benefit

## Legal contrast strategy

Create contrast inside the legitimate proposal:

- diagnose the real problem in the opening
- show one directly relevant case
- present a concise execution plan
- identify one risk the client may have missed
- offer a minimal and a recommended scope when useful
- attach a short video or demo for high-value opportunities

## A/B testing strategy

- Generate two or three internal proposal variants.
- Score them for specificity, truthfulness, proof and risk.
- Send only the strongest version from the legitimate account.
- Test different variants across comparable projects over time.
- Track replies, qualified replies, funded projects and net value.

## Broad-direction strategy

One account can present a coherent umbrella offer while each proposal stays narrow and relevant.

Profile umbrella:

`Antonov Digital — AI, automation, web systems and digital production`

Internal service lanes choose the relevant case and language for each project.
