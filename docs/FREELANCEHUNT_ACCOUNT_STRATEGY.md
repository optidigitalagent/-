# Freelancehunt Account Strategy

## Confirmed operating model

Freelancehunt support provided two separate decisions on 2026-08-31.

### Minor founder account

The founder’s account registered at age 15 is not an operational channel.

Support confirmed that it cannot be used for any purpose before age 18, including passive project alerts and market analysis. The account must be deactivated and may be restored through support after the founder turns 18.

Status:

`BLOCKED_UNTIL_18_DEACTIVATION_REQUIRED`

Required actions:

- Deactivate the account through security settings.
- Keep it disconnected from Gmail, Telegram, CRM and all automation.
- Do not complete the profile, verify identity, buy a plan, browse projects, receive alerts, send messages or submit bids.
- Do not register a replacement account for the same minor founder using another mailbox.

### Adult-cofounder account

Support confirmed that a genuine adult cofounder may independently register and operate their own account.

Support also stated that it sees no problem with identifying the minor founder in client correspondence as a partner because the legal account owner is a legally capable adult.

Status:

`APPROVED_ADULT_OWNER_WITH_DISCLOSED_PARTNER`

Mandatory structure:

- The adult cofounder registers with their own truthful identity and personally controlled mailbox.
- The adult cofounder is the sole controller of the account login.
- The adult cofounder passes verification.
- The adult cofounder submits bids and conducts platform correspondence.
- The adult cofounder contracts with clients and receives platform payments.
- The minor founder does not log in, operate the account or write while impersonating the adult owner.
- The minor founder may be truthfully introduced in correspondence as an Antonov Digital partner.
- The team must describe roles, responsibility and delivery participation truthfully to each client.
- Platform rules on automation remain unchanged; the approval does not permit automatic bidding, mass messaging or credential sharing.

## Profile ownership and brand presentation

The account is the adult cofounder’s real freelancer account, not a corporate shared login.

Antonov Digital may be presented as the delivery brand only in a way that remains truthful about:

- the adult account owner’s identity
- the two-founder team
- the minor founder’s partner role
- who communicates on-platform
- who is contractually responsible
- who will perform each part of the work

Until exact profile fields are inspected, do not force team wording into fields intended only for an individual’s legal identity.

## Rejected approach

Do not create a second fake or low-quality account to submit a weak proposal before the main account.

Reasons:

- prohibited duplicate-account and truthfulness risk
- potential loss of accounts and funds
- coordinated deception of the client
- fragmented rating, reviews and portfolio trust
- additional subscriptions, verification and operational complexity
- no reliable incremental conversion benefit

## Legal contrast strategy

Create contrast inside one legitimate proposal:

- diagnose the real problem in the opening
- show one directly relevant case
- present a concise execution plan
- identify one risk the client may have missed
- offer a minimal and a recommended scope when useful
- attach a short video or demo for high-value opportunities

## A/B testing strategy

- Generate two or three internal proposal variants.
- Score them for specificity, truthfulness, proof and risk.
- Send only the strongest version through the adult-owned account.
- Test different variants across comparable opportunities over time.
- Track replies, qualified replies, funded projects and net value.

## Broad-direction strategy

The adult-owned account can present a coherent umbrella offer while each proposal stays narrow and relevant.

Profile umbrella:

`Antonov Digital — AI, automation, web systems and digital production`

Internal service lanes choose the relevant case and language for each project.
