## Case template

Each public case must include:

1. Client or anonymized context.
2. Business problem.
3. Exact role of Antonov Digital.
4. What was built.
5. Technologies and integrations.
6. What can be demonstrated now.
7. Measured result, or an explicit statement that outcome data was not collected.
8. Screenshots, live link or sanitized demo.
9. Relevant service tags.

Never invent performance metrics.
