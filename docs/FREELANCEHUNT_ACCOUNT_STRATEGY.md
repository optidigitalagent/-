Current status:

`BLOCKED_UNTIL_18_DEACTIVATION_REQUIRED`

- Deactivate the account through security settings.
- Keep it disconnected from Gmail, Telegram, CRM and all automation.

## Possible adult-founder route

- communicator with clients
- contracting party
- payment recipient

The account must not be shared, borrowed or used to impersonate the adult owner.

Because the support reply did not explicitly answer whether Antonov Digital may disclose and involve a minor founder as part of the delivery team, this route remains:

`PENDING_EXPLICIT_PLATFORM_APPROVAL`

Do not launch it until support confirms the exact permitted team format in writing.

## Rejected approach

Do not create a second fake or low-quality account to submit a weak proposal before the main account.

Reasons:

- prohibited duplicate-account and truthfulness risk
- potential loss of accounts and funds
- coordinated deception of the client
- fragmented rating, reviews and portfolio trust
- additional subscriptions, verification and operational complexity
- no reliable incremental conversion benefit

## Legal contrast strategy

For any future approved account, create contrast inside one legitimate proposal:

- diagnose the real problem in the opening
- show one directly relevant case
- present a concise execution plan
- identify one risk the client may have missed
- offer a minimal and a recommended scope when useful
- attach a short video or demo for high-value opportunities

## A/B testing strategy

- Generate two or three internal proposal variants.
- Score them for specificity, truthfulness, proof and risk.
- Send only the strongest version through the approved account/channel.
- Test different variants across comparable opportunities over time.
- Track replies, qualified replies, funded projects and net value.
