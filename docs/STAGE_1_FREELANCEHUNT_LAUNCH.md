# Stage 1 — Freelancehunt Launch

## Objective

Make one legitimate adult-owned Freelancehunt account capable of winning work quickly while minimizing manual sales effort.

Stage 1 ends only when:

1. the adult-owned account is active and truthfully presented;
2. the profile, categories and portfolio are strong enough to support proposals;
3. Freelancehunt notification emails reach the operational Gmail mailbox;
4. new opportunities are surfaced in Telegram quickly;
5. the system produces a tailored proposal draft for each qualified opportunity;
6. the adult account owner can submit the final platform action without building the proposal from scratch.

## Operating model

- Sole active account: genuine adult owner.
- Operational mailbox: `PLATFORM_OWNER_MAILBOX_ADDRESS`.
- The adult owner controls login, verification, bids, platform messages, contracts and payments.
- Artem is disclosed truthfully as an Antonov Digital co-founder/partner where relevant.
- Vadim is described truthfully as a developer when he participates in delivery.
- No second active Freelancehunt account or alert mirror is used in the current launch.

## Truthful high-conversion rule

Optimize every public field and proposal for what the client needs, but do not invent verifiable facts.

Allowed:

- strong positioning around real capabilities;
- concrete delivery promises that the team can fulfill;
- custom plans for the client’s task;
- demos and internal projects clearly presented as demos/internal work;
- team-based delivery explanations;
- relevant case selection;
- aggressive but realistic speed and scope framing.

Not allowed:

- false age or identity;
- fabricated clients, testimonials, revenue, rankings or project outcomes;
- fake years of experience or completed-project counts;
- presenting a demo as paid client work;
- claiming a technology or delivery capacity without evidence or a realistic execution path.

The commercial standard is: sound like the best suitable executor because the proposal proves understanding, plan, speed and capability—not because it invents history.

## Stage 1 workstreams

### 1. Adult account activation

- Register or use the sole adult-owned account on the operational mailbox.
- Adult owner completes login, phone/identity verification, CAPTCHA and 2FA.
- Do not use or reactivate the deactivated minor-owned account.

### 2. Profile conversion layer

Complete:

- real adult owner identity fields;
- Ukrainian primary public copy;
- Antonov Digital positioning;
- broad truthful service coverage;
- project/milestone pricing or negotiable pricing;
- full availability;
- Ukrainian, Russian and English communication;
- Polish written communication only when AI-assisted and accurately described;
- real owner photo;
- strongest evidence-backed portfolio items.

Browser automation is optional. It must not block launch. If Codex cannot reliably edit the live UI, use the prepared field-by-field copy and complete the reversible fields manually.

### 3. Category and alert coverage

- Select the broadest truthful main and additional categories supported by the plan.
- Enable project digests and all relevant client-message/account notifications.
- Confirm the sender and recipient identity after the adult account is active.
- Treat old mail associated with another account identity as historical data only.

### 4. Gmail visibility

The operational Gmail mailbox is the source of truth for permitted email notifications.

Two access modes:

- ChatGPT connector: on-demand search/read when requested in chat.
- Railway/Telegram monitor: automatic near-real-time notification delivery.

ChatGPT does not proactively send a message merely because a new email arrived unless a separate automation is running.

### 5. Opportunity card

For every new project, create:

- title and source link;
- full available task description;
- language;
- budget and deadline;
- service lane;
- fit and delivery risk;
- proposed project/milestone price;
- realistic delivery time;
- best matching portfolio evidence;
- one selected tailored proposal;
- one short next action for the adult owner.

### 6. Proposal standard

Each proposal must be adapted to the actual task and contain:

1. direct understanding of the requested result;
2. a concise implementation plan;
3. one relevant proof point or clearly labelled demo;
4. realistic scope, price and timeline;
5. one useful clarification only when needed;
6. a low-friction call to action.

Generate multiple variants internally when useful, but submit only the strongest truthful version.

## Automation boundary

Automatic:

- Gmail alert ingestion;
- extraction and normalization;
- category and fit scoring;
- proposal drafting;
- Telegram/CRM notification;
- follow-up reminders outside prohibited platform automation;
- metrics and experiment tracking.

Adult-owner platform action:

- login and verification;
- submitting bids;
- sending platform messages;
- accepting contractual terms;
- payments and withdrawals.

## Acceptance test

Stage 1 is operational after a controlled test proves:

1. a Freelancehunt notification reaches the operational Gmail account;
2. the monitor detects it within the target interval;
3. Telegram receives a complete opportunity card;
4. the proposed response matches the task and contains no invented claims;
5. the adult owner can submit it with minimal editing;
6. reply emails and important account notices are not missed.
